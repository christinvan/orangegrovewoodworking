About

With a strong background in construction as a carpenter, Bryce VanLente discovered his true passion in the intricate art of woodworking. His transition from large-scale construction to detailed craftsmanship has given him a unique perspective on both structural integrity and aesthetic beauty.

As a Florida native, Bryce understands the unique needs of Southwest Florida homes and the importance of using materials and techniques suited to our climate. His amazing attention to detail ensures that every project, whether it's a simple cutting board or an elaborate built-in library, receives the same level of dedication and precision.

At Orange Grove Woodworking, we believe that wood tells a story, and we're honored to help write yours through custom pieces that will be cherished for generations.
